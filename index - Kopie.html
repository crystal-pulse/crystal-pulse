Installation Guide

To use Crystal Pulse, you will need either Vencord or BetterDiscord installed. Please make sure to visit their official websites if you haven't installed them yet! ❤️

Step 1
Open Discord → Vencord → Themes → Local Themes → Open Themes Folder.
Step 2
Double click on Download Crystal Pulse on the main page to save the theme file.
Step 3
Drag and drop the downloaded file directly into the themes folder you opened in Step 1.
Step 4
Open Discord → Settings → Themes → Local Themes → Load Missing Themes → Click on the toggle/cross next to Crystal Pulse to activate it! ✨
